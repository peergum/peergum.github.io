I built Marillion Fans Canada, an unofficial, bilingual community site for Canadian fans of the band.

The site brings together community discussions, band and music information, news, upcoming announcements, and an e-zine. It uses a Go backend, PostgreSQL, and a React frontend. The public site is currently on hold.
