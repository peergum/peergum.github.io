I built The Bookshelf, a self-hosted platform for publishing and reading novels and comics on the web.

Authors can create and manage works in a Studio, arrange chapters and comic pages, and publish them for readers. The mobile-friendly reader supports prose and paged comics, with notes for readers and authors. The application uses Go, PostgreSQL, and React.
