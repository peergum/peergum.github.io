---
title: Paris (1993-1997)
order: 3
---
Now 26, I discovered I was going to be a father, and quickly got a pretty job as the assistant to the Network Manager in a company of a thousand employees spread between Paris and European branches. My daughter was born in the summer of 1994.

In this first job, I learned everything that the theory from schools had not taught me, and quickly became a highly respected actor in the company, responsible for network operations, locally and remotely.

A new member was added to the family in 1997, my son, right before I eventually moved to the South of France for another job, having exhausted the options for me to grow inside my current company.
