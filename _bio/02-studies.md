title: Studies (1984-1993)
order: 2
---
Paris was a huge change to me, and being among the smartest in my home town only meant being an average student in 11th and 12th grade, in a class mostly composed by highly competitive adolescents that eventually ended in the best schools in the country.

My ambitions and confidence were hurt, but on my 18th year, I still entered Math Sup / Math Spé, preparatory classes for engineering schools, a hyper competitive and quite demoralizing environment where many eventually desist, and take the path of more classic universities.

I eventually was accepted in a decent electrical engineering school in Rouen, Normandy, called ESIGELEC, where I developed my theoretical and practical knowledge of electricity, physics, systems, networks, and software development. I graduated in 1992 with a major in systems and networks.

Back to Paris, I did a one year additional Master in Information Systems, that not only covered technical knowledge, but also business aspects like finance or marketing, and graduated in 1993.
