Little after eventually obtaining my Canadian citizenship, and starting some remote work with a small business in Waterloo, Ontario, my new partner, Sapna, and I started the trip we had planned for months, riding 2-up on a motorcycle from Vancouver, BC, to Buenos Aires, Argentina, through the US, Mexico, Central America and finally South America.

The trip took us 19 months overall, and led us through amazing places. You can follow the blogging Sapna did during our trip, and the videos, at [Riding Around The World](https://r-atw.com).

Back to BC in early 2019, we eventually decided to move to Kitchener in Ontario, the neighbouring city to Waterloo and the small business I had continued to work with for 2 years now.
