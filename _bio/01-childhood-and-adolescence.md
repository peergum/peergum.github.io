---
title: Childhood and adolescence (1968-1984)
order: 1
---
I was born in 1968 in Epinal, a small town in the East of France, where my parents moved to after finishing their studies in Paris. Both are engineers, now retired. I have two brothers, and I'm the middle kid.

My childhood was nothing particular, but I soon happened to always be the best kid in my classes, the untold favorite of my teachers, occasionally competing with one or two other classmates for the best scores in all tests. I remember the teacher giving me drawings to do, just because I always finished my tests some time before the others. The thing they also all concurred with is, I was quite the Chatty Cathy ("pipelette" in French), and I don't think this has changed much.

One of my two best friends from elementary school is still living in Epinal, where he's now a medical doctor, and the other one followed an entrepreneur path that eventually led him to New Caledonia later on.

My parents' divorce when I was around 10 years old, and my brothers and I eventually moved with my mother and stepfather to Paris in 1984, while my father followed an international career around the world.
