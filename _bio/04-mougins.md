I lived in Mougins for around one year, before moving to Grasse. Both cities were 15 minutes away from Sophia-Antipolis, where SITA/Equant, my new employer was located.

SITA is the company that manages airport communications worldwide, providing them the network and messaging infrastructure. Equant is the commercial entity that was born out of it, providing the same infrastructure to commercial customers, inclusive large insurance and banks worldwide.

My role there, in the Network Center of Excellence, was initially to work on operational services like internal DNS and network monitoring servers, but quickly expanded to providing additional tools to the network experts that helped them troubleshoot the issues happening on the 25000 X25 and Frame-Relay routers.

In 1999, I eventually moved to Atlanta to build the same team we had in Nice.
